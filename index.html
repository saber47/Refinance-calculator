Investment-adjusted break-even

Interest-based break-even compares the future value of each month's mortgage interest savings with the future value of upfront refinancing fees. It is distinct from payment-based break-even, and does not account for principal-balance differences. For financed fees, the interest comparison includes the extra interest on those fees, and there is no upfront fee to recover.
